# Neon Genie Input Modes (v0.4 canonical)

- PROMPT_LED: Free-form or structured prompt seeds.
- MARKET_LED: Evidence of market gaps, trends, or demand.
- CAPABILITY_LED: Existing Abraxas or repo capabilities as starting point.
- CONSTRAINT_LED: Sell-first or tight constraints (budget, time, platform).
- TREND_LED: Emerging patterns or cultural/technical shifts.
- PAINPOINT_LED: Specific user or operator friction.
- SYMBOLIC_LED: Motifs or seeds from Noctis (SHADOW symbolic only).
- MONETIZATION_LED: Revenue model or packaging first.

All modes must produce an EvidencePack (or explicit NOT_COMPUTABLE) before evidence-backed claims.
