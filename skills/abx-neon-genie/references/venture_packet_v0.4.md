# NeonGenieVenturePacket.v0.4 (conceptual minimum)

Required fields (repo-standard equivalent):
- input_mode
- seed_evidence (EvidencePack refs or NOT_COMPUTABLE)
- venture_name
- output_class (from allowed list)
- target_user
- pain_point
- mechanism
- core_output
- why_now_basis (OBSERVED/INFERRED/SPECULATIVE/NOT_COMPUTABLE labeled)
- market_model (evidence-backed or NOT_COMPUTABLE)
- build_model
- score_vector (deterministic)
- overlap_classification (NOVEL | ADJACENT | WRAPPER_CANDIDATE | CHILD_SUBSYSTEM | DUPLICATE | NOT_COMPUTABLE)
- route_binding
- readiness
- blocked_by
- provenance (input hashes, runner, skill version, timestamp)

Public packages must redact internal architecture, runes, canon language, governance internals, and private evidence.
